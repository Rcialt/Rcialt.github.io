Radiant Floor Heating in Grand County, CO

Nothing beats warm floors when it's thirty below outside. We install in-floor radiant heat in new builds, basements, garages, and shops.

Get a quote

Why Radiant Wins in the Mountains

Forced air heats the ceiling first and stirs up dust doing it. Radiant heats the floor you actually stand on, holds steady, even warmth through long cold snaps, and doesn't blink during a windstorm. In a climate with a seven-month heating season, that comfort difference is dramatic — and so is the efficiency.

Radiant also frees your design: no ducts to hide, no registers in the wrong wall, and every square foot of floor usable.

Where We Install It

The best time for radiant is when concrete is being poured — tubing goes in the slab, and the whole thermal mass becomes the heater. Because we pour our own foundations and slabs, radiant integrates into our projects seamlessly.

  • In-slab radiant for new homes, basements, and additions
  • Heated garage and shop slabs
  • Underfloor systems for framed floors in remodels
  • Snow-melt considerations for entries and walks
  • Boiler and mechanical coordination with licensed partners

Plan It Early

Radiant is cheap to add before the pour and expensive to add after. If you're planning a build, addition, garage, or basement finish anywhere in Grand County, tell us early that you want warm floors — we'll design the slab, insulation, and zoning for it from day one.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add radiant heat to an existing home?

Yes — underfloor systems can be retrofitted below framed floors, and basement or garage slabs can get radiant when they're replaced or newly poured. In-slab during new construction is still the gold standard.

Is radiant heat enough on its own at this altitude?

A properly designed system heats a well-insulated mountain home comfortably through the coldest snaps. Design is the key word — insulation under the slab, correct tube spacing, and zoning all matter, and we plan those with the structure.

Does a heated garage slab cost much to run?

Kept at a modest temperature, an insulated radiant garage slab is efficient — the mass holds heat, and you're not blasting a unit heater every time you open the door. Insulation under and around the slab is what makes the difference, and we never skip it.
